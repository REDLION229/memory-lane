YouTube Memory Lane

See what you watched and said on YouTube, years ago.

Import the copy of your YouTube data that Google lets you download. It's read right here on this computer and never uploaded anywhere.

Select every Takeout .zip at once. No need to unzip. You can also drag files anywhere on this page.

Don't have your data yet?

Get it from Google in 3 steps

About 2 minutes, plus waiting for Google's email
  1. 01

    Request it

    1. Open Google Takeout and sign in.
    2. Click Deselect all, then scroll down and tick YouTube and YouTube Music.
    3. Click All YouTube data included. Keep these ticked and untick the rest:
      commentshistorylive chatsmessagesvideo metadata
    4. Click Next step, keep Export once and .zip, then click Create export.
    Open Google Takeout
  2. 02

    Download it

    Google emails you when it's ready. That's usually within an hour, but it can take a day. Open the email, click Download your files, and download every part if there's more than one.

  3. 03

    Import it here

    Click Import your YouTube data and select all the .zip files together. Your memories show up right away, and they'll still be here next time you open the app.

Is this safe?

Yes. The app runs entirely in your browser and your files never leave this computer. The only optional online feature looks up the names of videos your export doesn't include, which sends just the video's ID to YouTube. You can switch it off after importing.

My download is huge (many gigabytes)

That's your uploaded videos. You can still import it: the app skips videos and only reads the small files it needs. For a much smaller download next time, untick videos in step 1.

Google split it into several .zip files

Download all of them and select them together when importing. You can also add more later with Add files at the top of the page.

Where is my data kept after importing?

In this browser, so it opens instantly next time. Click Start over at the top of the page to erase it. Your original files are never changed.
