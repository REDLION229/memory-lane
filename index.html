Drop your Google Takeout here

A .zip, an unzipped folder, or single files. Everything stays on your computer.
Don't have your export yet?
  1. Go to takeout.google.com, click Deselect all, and tick YouTube and YouTube Music.
  2. Under All YouTube data included, keep comments, history, live chats and messages. Untick videos, they're huge.
  3. Optionally, under Multiple formats, set history to JSON.
  4. Export once as .zip, download it when Google emails you, then drop it here.
If Takeout split your export into several zips, select all of them at once.
